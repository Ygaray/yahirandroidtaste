## Bucket A — Visual judgments that need your eyes (Phase 10, light AND dark)

The *mechanisms* below are all already confirmed working live (see Phase 10 Gate-1 evidence,
`10-02-SELF-UAT.md`). What is deferred to you is purely the **"does it look / read / feel good"** call.

### A1 — ProviderKeyCard: masked-key reveal contrast/readability
- **Look at:** the masked API-key field (`••••…`) and the reveal (eye) toggle, in both themes.
- **Expected:** key masked by default; tapping the eye flips to the raw key legibly; the dots and the
  revealed text both read cleanly against the card background in light and dark; the eye icon is an
  obvious, discoverable affordance.
- **Your call:** contrast + readability quality of masked vs revealed states, both themes.

### A2 — ApproachLadderCard: cap interaction feel + hidden-vs-shown-disabled layout
- **Look at:** tap a rung to set the max-tier **cap**; watch the "Capped" tag appear on the rung(s)
  above; compare a card with controls present vs the "every control hidden (null props)" variant cell.
- **Expected:** tapping a rung feels discoverable and responsive; a capped rung renders visibly
  greyed-but-present (not removed); the all-null variant shows **no** toggle row and **no** cap tags at
  uniform full opacity (controls genuinely absent, not disabled-looking).
- **Your call:** interaction feel/discoverability of tap-a-rung, and layout quality of
  hidden-control vs shown-disabled, both themes.

### A3 — Combined subdued-label legibility (now a LIVE gallery cell)
- **Look at:** the new cell **"ApproachLadderCard — combined subdued labels (Unavailable + Capped +
  Needs network)"** in the ApproachLadderCard variants — its top ("Cloud") rung carries **all three**
  subdued labels at once. Review in both themes.
- **Expected:** all three labels present, distinct, and reading well together without crowding or
  contrast loss. (Gate-1's addendum already proved they render structurally intact and independently
  gated; what remains is only the "does it read well" judgment.)
- **Your call:** combined legibility/contrast of the three stacked labels, both themes.
- **⚠ Prep flagged two things to look at here** (observed during capture, *not* adjudicated — your call):
  - The three subdued labels pack **tightly toward the card's right edge** ("Needs network" sits very
    close to the edge) in both themes — judge the breathing room.
  - In **light** theme the capped "Cloud" rung *label* reads **less obviously dimmed** than its
    siblings (the subdued cue is carried mostly by the trailing labels, not the rung-label tone); it's
    clearer in dark. Judge whether the light-theme subdued treatment is strong enough.
