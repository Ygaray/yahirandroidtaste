**Locked by Yahir 2026-10-17** (via director@office, "I like A" + "All recs"). Design source:
`docs/design/snackbar-go-to-item/index.html` (the options page). Client: teamlead@vae-bilingual.
Consumer input: SecondBrain `.planning/cross-repo/SNACKBAR-NAV-SURVEY.md` (gaps G1-G3 are YAT's).

## Locked decisions

- **D-01 Direction A:** Receipt-card cascading deck. `surfaceContainerHighest` card, 16dp corners,
  tone dot, message + optional second line (item name), Undo as a text action in `primary`. Up to 3
  visible; older cards tuck behind the newest (peek ~14dp up, scale 0.94/0.88). Tapping the peek fans
  the deck out into a column; it re-collapses after idle.
- **D-02 No action label:** tapping the snackbar body opens the referenced item. Undo stays where present.
- **D-03 Chevron:** a trailing › icon on snackbars that have a target, none otherwise. Icon, not text.
- **D-04 Timeouts unchanged:** Short 4 s, Long 10 s, through
  `AccessibilityManager.calculateRecommendedTimeoutMillis` (containsControls when Undo or a target).
- **D-05 Pause:** each timer pauses while a pointer is down on a snackbar or the deck is fanned out;
  resumes on release / collapse.
- **D-06 Stack cap:** a 4th arrival dismisses the oldest early (result Dismissed); WithUndo entries
  stay in the Undo Center.
- **D-07 Swipe:** horizontal swipe dismisses one snackbar (Dismissed); never undoes.
- **D-08 Target model:** `FeedbackTarget(key: String, openLabel: String)`; opaque consumer-encoded key,
  localized TalkBack click label. Not a lambda (singleton `UndoHistoryStore` must not hold a NavController).
- **D-09 Additive API:** `target: FeedbackTarget? = null` appended to `FeedbackEvent.Notify`,
  `FeedbackEvent.WithUndo`, `VoiceOutcomeUiState.Success`, `BatchRowResultUiModel`, `UndoHistoryEntry`
  (internal ctor). New `FeedbackStackState` / `rememberFeedbackStackState(maxVisible = 3)` with
  `suspend fun show(event): FeedbackResult` (Opened | Undone | Dismissed | TimedOut);
  `FeedbackStackHost(state, onOpenTarget, modifier, undoLabel = "Undo", dismissLabel = "Dismiss")`;
  `FeedbackStackState.emitTrackedWithUndo(store, event)`. `UndoCenterScreen` overload with
  `onOpenTarget`. `OutcomeSheet(..., onOpenTarget = null)` on both overloads, Receipt AND Classic.
  Existing `SnackbarHostState.emitTrackedWithUndo` + M3 hosts keep working unchanged.
- **D-10 Sheet go-to-item:** label-less: the receipt row naming the item (Success.target) and each
  `BatchRowResultUiModel` with a target becomes a tap target with the chevron; no target = no chevron,
  not clickable. After Undo / Undo all, the item loses its target (library-side once the entry is Undone).
- **D-11 Accessibility:** snackbar body is one merged node, `Role.Button`, `onClickLabel = openLabel`,
  `liveRegion = Polite`; Undo stays a separate focusable child and is also a custom action; "Dismiss"
  custom action + `dismiss` semantics (swipe equivalent); deck items stay in the tree newest-first.
- **D-12 Not in scope:** combined "Undo & open" for soft-deleted items; app-side routing; share/MCP
  surfaces; the SecondBrain sheet-swap blink (app-side).

## Designer decisions (ui-designer, 2026-10-17, answering the roadmapper's open questions)

- **D-13 DismissScreen in `show()`:** rendered like `Notify` (message only, no target, Short); the
  "dismiss/navigate back" stays the consumer dispatcher's job, as today. Result follows the normal
  rules (TimedOut / Dismissed).
- **D-14 Losing the target after Undo:** snackbars and Undo Center rows derive it in the library
  from the store entry's status (Undone/Failed → not openable). `VoiceOutcomeUiState.Success` and
  `BatchRowResultUiModel` are caller-built state, so the CALLER clears `target` after Undo / Undo
  all; KDoc says so. (The library has no link from a Success to store entries.)
- **D-15 Which Success row opens:** when `Success.target != null`, the sheet renders the target as a
  dedicated item row under the title (ReceiptRow-style: `target.openLabel` is NOT shown; the row
  shows the Success `summary` as its title, with a trailing chevron) and the whole row is the tap
  target. Classic gets the same row under its summary. No target → layout identical to v2.7.1.
- **D-16 POL-02 bar:** 3:1 non-text contrast (WCAG 1.4.11) for the dot against the pill, in light,
  dark and dynamic schemes.
- **D-17 Phase 25 tracer host:** Phase 25 lands `FeedbackStackHost` with basic newest-first
  rendering so the registry drift guard holds; Phase 26 owns the deck look, gestures, timers, a11y.
