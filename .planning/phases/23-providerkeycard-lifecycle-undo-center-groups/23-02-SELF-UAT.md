## Notes / anomalies (for the Gate-2 reviewer and the orchestrator)

1. **ADV-1 (cosmetic, owner/UX call, not a criterion failure): a long group label is ellipsized with no way to read the rest.** A 75-character label (`Imported 14 cards from the Monday research clipboard and linked their tags`) next to the German Undo all button at 360 dp wraps to two lines and cuts at `the Monday research …` (screen 79). If the owner wants the full label recoverable (three lines, or the button stacked under the text), it is a one-parameter change in `UndoGroupHeaderRow`.
2. **ADV-2 (UX note, not a criterion): `inFlight = true` locks the actions and the provider dropdown but leaves the key text field editable** (capture 23, key field `enabled=true`). The spec says every ACTION is disabled, which holds; whether typing should also lock during a save is a product call.
