<objective>
Implement the domain-vocabulary drift guard (GOV-02) and document the tier-aware contribution litmus
it partially enforces (GOV-01). Per locked decisions D-02/D-03 (03-CONTEXT.md), the guard is a
fail-until-allowlisted JUnit test — reusing `ComponentRegistryDriftGuardTest`'s source-text-scan shape
(duplicated, not imported — the extraction methods are `private` on that class) — that flags any
public top-level `@Composable` whose leading PascalCase word ("head token", RESEARCH.md Open Question
2's corroborated reading) is not in a seed `PRIMITIVE_NOUN_ALLOWLIST` of the hub's own established
UI-archetype nouns, unless the full name is explicitly acknowledged (with rationale) in a new
`DOMAIN_VOCABULARY` map mirroring `INTENTIONALLY_UNREGISTERED`'s shape. Per the Runtime Decision
refresh of D-04, `docs/DESIGN-INTENT.md` (not a new `CONTRIBUTING.md`) gets two new sections stating
the litmus and its enforcement split.

Purpose: surface — without forbidding — every future public composable that couples the hub to
consumer-domain vocabulary, so it gets human review instead of silently drifting in.
Output: `DomainVocabularyDriftGuardTest.kt` (new, green against the live corpus with its day-one
grandfather list seeded), and `docs/DESIGN-INTENT.md` extended with the litmus + enforcement sections.

a single tracer slice could cross. This plan pairs GOV-02 (the guard) with GOV-01 (the doc describing
it) specifically because D-04's own text ties them together ("enforced where feasible" = the GOV-02
guard) — writing the doc after/alongside the guard lets its enforcement claim be accurate against
what was actually built, which is the closest analog to an end-to-end slice this pairing has.

**Reversibility check:** a new JUnit test file and two appended doc sections are fully additive and
reversible (delete the file, revert the doc diff) — no published-contract break, no migration,
`git revert` fully undoes either. No `checkpoint:decision` is warranted.
