<objective>
Add the `Tier` enum and the required `Entry.tier` field to `ComponentRegistry` (D-01), then apply
the D-03 litmus to tier all 11 Cards-family and all 5 Chips-family entries — the first real slice
of the registry-wide tiering sweep this phase requires.

Purpose: `Entry.tier` is a required (no-default) field on a `data class` instantiated at 53 call
sites across 9 files in the SAME Kotlin compilation unit — the whole module will not compile again
until every one of the 53 sites supplies `tier = ...`. This plan is the tracer: it lands the field
itself and proves the tiering mechanism (read the component's real signature, apply the two-question
litmus, assign the value) on the two families already worked through in RESEARCH.md/PATTERNS.md
(Cards, including the CardBase borderline case, and Chips, including the ChipBar borderline case).
Because the field is required with no default, this plan's own build CANNOT go fully green yet —
7 more families still lack `tier =` at this point (they land in `01-03-PLAN.md`/`01-04-PLAN.md`,
Wave 2) — so this plan's own verification is source-level (grep-confirmed), not a full
`./gradlew compileDebugKotlin`. That full green build is deferred to `01-05-PLAN.md` (Wave 3),
which is the first point ALL 53 sites exist together. This is a hard compiler constraint of a
required, no-default field on a class instantiated across the whole module — not an oversight.

Output: `ComponentRegistry.Tier` enum + `Entry.tier` field; 16 of 53 entries (Cards + Chips) tiered.
</objective>
