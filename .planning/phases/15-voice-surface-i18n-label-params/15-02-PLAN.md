<objective>
Make the OutcomeSheet's undo-surface literals caller-overridable through additive, defaulted model
fields (VI18N-04, part 1 of 2): `UndoRowUiModel.undoneLabel` ("Undone", D-02 per-row) and
`UndoRefusedUiModel.refusedPrefix` ("Couldn't undo:") + `changedSinceSuffix` ("changed since").
`HandledByUiModel` and `ProposedItemUiModel` follow in plan 15-03.

This is the phase's riskiest edit: appending a field to a shipped `data class` is NOT additive to
Metalava by default (RESEARCH Critical Finding — naive appends produced 8 `RemovedMethod` errors on the
constructors and `copy`). The recipe, reproduced green in a scratch copy, is `@JvmOverloads constructor`
plus a hand-written old-arity `copy` overload that delegates to the generated full-arity `copy`.
Task 1 is the tracer that proves the whole recipe on the smallest model (`UndoRowUiModel`) through
every layer — model, OutcomeSheet render, JVM + Compose tests, `apiCheck`, `apiDump`, lane-3 commit.
Task 2 repeats it for `UndoRefusedUiModel` (two fields) and closes the wave with the full suite.

Purpose: SecondBrain can localize the "Undone" row text and the refused-undo message without any
behavior change for callers that pass none of the new fields; `UndoRowState.Undone` stays a data object.
Output: two re-shaped data classes (additive), OutcomeSheet reading the new fields, a new pure-JVM model
test file, new OutcomeSheetTest override tests, two regenerated api.txt commits, a green full suite.
</objective>
