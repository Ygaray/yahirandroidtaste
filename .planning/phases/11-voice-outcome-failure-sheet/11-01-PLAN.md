members via `UndoHistoryEntry.tryConsume()` (a one-way `AtomicBoolean` CAS with no release path)
would permanently strand a member after a `Refused` retry. The group claim is therefore guarded
entirely by a new `groupUndoMutex` (`kotlinx.coroutines.sync.Mutex`, already transitively available
-- no new Gradle dependency), never `tryConsume()`.
