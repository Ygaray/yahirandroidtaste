## Method notes (read these before the verdicts)

- **Speech was captured, so most "spoken-only" parking items are verified, not parked.** TalkBack (Samsung 15.1.01.1, already on the device) was enabled only through the secure settings together with a throwaway observer AccessibilityService. TalkBack's developer setting "Display speech output" draws each phrase it speaks in an overlay; I focused or clicked nodes through accessibility actions and grabbed screencaps back to back (about 0.3 s apart), OCR-ed the overlay box (tesseract, umlauts sometimes lost) and kept the transcript with time offsets plus the observer events and the TTS utterance durations of the same window (Samsung TTS logs the text encrypted, so durations only). One transcript per file, evidence `tb-*` (69 to 114), method and limits in 115. The frames themselves are working copies, not evidence; the transcripts, one overlay montage (68) and the signed observer dumps are.
- **What could not be driven:** one-finger swipes (next/previous, heading navigation) and keyboard shortcuts of TalkBack. `input swipe`, `input touchscreen swipe`, `input keycombination` and `input keyboard keycombination` were not recognised (a swipe over the sheet scrim simply dismissed the sheet; transcripts 74, 80, 81, 82). Those sub-steps are the physical ones below.
- **uiautomator dump suppresses every other accessibility service while it runs** (the observer and TalkBack rebind afterwards), so navigation used uiautomator with TalkBack off and speech observations used TalkBack on. The Samsung freezer froze the observer process a few seconds after its last binder call until the observer called `startForeground`; its first builds also coalesced events with `notificationTimeout=20`, so the C-1 event counts below come from the final build (`notificationTimeout=0`).
- **A uiautomator `tapb`/`tap` helper picked a wrong node twice** (a German label substring matched an English one; an apostrophe broke the shell quoting of one focus request). Both were noticed from the output, fixed and re-run; the transcripts of the wrong-node trials are in the evidence and are called out where they matter.
