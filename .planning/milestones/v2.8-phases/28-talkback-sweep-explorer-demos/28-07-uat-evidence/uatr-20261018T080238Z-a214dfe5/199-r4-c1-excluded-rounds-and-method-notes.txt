# Phase 28 Gate-1 re-verification 3, C-1: rounds that are NOT valid showings, and what I learned about driving TalkBack alone

A showing is valid only if the observation window (logcat cleared, overlay frames from the trigger) contains the sheet APPEARANCE. In this TalkBack
(Samsung 15.1.01.1) an injected `input tap` on the "Show sheet" button OPENS the sheet at the FIRST tap in several states (right after the BACK that
dismissed the previous showing; or right after a warm focus tap). So the arrangement of the earlier runs ("one real tap to focus, wait 6 s, two real taps")
often opened the sheet with the SETUP tap, before the window started, and then observed the aftermath of the second pair of taps landing on the open sheet.
The tally therefore counts only rounds whose transcript contains "Bottom Sheet".

Excluded rounds (kept as signed transcripts, none of them is a failure of the app):
- out-r4-c1-alone-1..4: first arrangement (setup tap + 6 s + two taps, window after the setup tap). Round 1 and 3 show the tail of a showing that began before the window (message in the overlay at t+0.4, then the hint); rounds 2 and 4 show nothing because the sheet was already open and the taps landed on it.
- out-r4-c1-alone1-1 and out-r4-c1-alone1-11: single tap on an already focused button; the sheet had opened earlier or did not open in the window; only the focus hint is spoken.
- out-r4-c1-alone2-11 (and the first, overwritten, non-background alone2-1 run): two quick taps while the sheet was already open (opened by the warm tap 5 s before); no speech at all in the window, which is what a tap on an open sheet region gives. I reproduced that sequence three times (repro-zero, repro3, and the original) and each time the preceding warm tap or the single tap had already opened the sheet; in repro3 the alone1 window caught the message at t+0.4 and the hint at t+3.5, i.e. the tail of the showing opened by the warm tap. A control with the same gestures on another demo sheet (out-r4-ctl-alone1/2) speaks "Bottom Sheet", "Drag handle" and the hint normally.
- out-r4-sil-alone1, out-r4-sil-alone2: the repro3 run described above.
- out-r4-probe-*: kept; the two probes whose windows contain the appearance are counted in the tally ("probe" group).

Explicit focus: with TalkBack alone, taps on the message node (45,1942 to 1035,2136) speak nothing (out-r4-c1-alone-explicit-focus-message-1..3): the node has no click action, an injected tap is a click, not a touch-exploration focus, and one-finger swipe navigation cannot be injected on this device (earlier runs). So the explicit-focus-by-swipe step is physical and stays a Gate-2 item. The explicit focus was driven with the observer bound (ACTION_ACCESSIBILITY_FOCUS on the node, out-r4-c1-explicit-focus-message-observer): TalkBack speaks the message ONCE (one 4.13 s utterance, encrypted text blndMGaYLtVK2Or4... identical to the utterance spoken at the sheet appearance) and no role ("Button" absent: no click action).
