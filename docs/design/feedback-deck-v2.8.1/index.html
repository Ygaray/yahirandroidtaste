FeedbackStackHost deck — v2.8.1 options (D-1683, Q-430)

Yahir's Gate-2 notes: (1) cards must stand out from the app background, (2) somewhat translucent, (3) side margins, not full-bleed. Common to every option: 16dp side margins (Dimens.HorizontalPadding, card still capped at 560dp, centered), 8dp below the deck so the shadow is not cut, and 92% group opacity: the deck is composited opaque, then faded as one layer, so back cards never show through the front card and the shadow does not darken the inside of a card. No backdrop blur: Compose has no behind-the-view blur without a new dependency.

The mock background is a busy CalTracker-style list. Each option is shown collapsed and fanned, light and dark. The numbers are computed from the hub scheme, worst case = 8% white or black bleeding through.

A — Inverse card (recommended)

M3 snackbar idiom: inverseSurface fill, inverseOnSurface text, inversePrimary dot + Undo. Card vs app background 12.4:1 light / 13.3:1 dark. Worst-case text over white/black bleed: 8.1:1; accent 4.3:1.

Recommendation

A. It is the only option that changes the fill enough to separate the card from any app background, in both themes (12:1 or more). B and C keep today's fill luminance and lean on a 1dp ring. The cost: it departs from the "Receipt card on surfaceContainerHighest" rule (STYLE §1, §5). Those lines get amended and the reason logged in STYLE.REVERTED.

Not in any option: a new public parameter. The patch changes the defaults for SB and CT. A hoisted color or opacity override can come later as an additive param if an app needs one.
